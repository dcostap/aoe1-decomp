### Callee identity and register use

Direct original-image inspection gives:

- Scrollbar constructor `0047A81F` stores vtable `00571830` into `[this]`.
- The dword at `00571830+0x14` is `00464DC0`, `TPanel::set_active(int)`.
- Its first instruction replaces EAX with the stack argument at `00464DC0`.
- It never uses incoming EDX as an input.
- Its parent-redraw branch assigns EDX from the parent's vptr at `00464DEE` before the indirect call.

For the zero argument, the callee either returns immediately when inactive, or stores zero and releases mouse capture.
`release_mouse` at `00466280` does not consume incoming EDX.
Its direct helpers `mouseOwner` and `setMouseOwner` use ECX and their specified stack arguments, not incoming EDX.
`ReleaseCapture` is a Win32 API with no EDX argument.
Any parent-redraw virtual call loads its own vptr before dispatch.

Therefore the differing incoming EAX is overwritten, and differing incoming EDX has no behavioral use.
Both registers are caller-saved under the established calling convention.
This is not an assumption that arbitrary register changes are safe.
