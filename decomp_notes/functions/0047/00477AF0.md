## 2026-09-10 fresh exact review

### Behavior

The function returns `0` when `input_pos >= input_len`. Otherwise it switches
the active double buffer, copies the prefix, and removes one character or a
line ending at the cursor. A CRLF pair removes two bytes. A LF preceded by CR
also moves `input_pos` back by one. It then copies the tail, updates
`input_len`, reformats text, recalculates the cursor, enables the caret, and
stores `debug_timeGetTime`.

The original reads `input_len` at `+0x1AC` and `input_pos` at `+0x1B4`.
It selects buffers at `+0x1A0`, `+0x1A4`, and `+0x1A8`. It writes
`input_len`, `draw_caret` at `+0x194`, and `caret_last_time` at `+0x190`.
The source preserves these widths and write order. It preserves the null and
empty paths shown by the original: no buffer dereference occurs before the
initial length test, but active buffers are then required.

All 14 calls have equal targets and argument order. They are eleven multibyte
cursor and copy calls, `reformat`, `calc_cur_line_col`, and
`debug_timeGetTime`. No callback or virtual call occurs in this body. Re-entry
through the two direct helpers remains an external concern. Malformed buffer
pointers, invalid cursor values, multibyte faults, and helper re-entry were
not runtime tested.
