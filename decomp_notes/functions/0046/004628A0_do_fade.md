## 004628A0 do_fade

The original source is `music.cpp`, lines 919 through 953.

The function updates fade volume from elapsed time. It stops playback at the lower volume limit. It restores normal volume and restarts playback when the fade ends.

The `250.0f` and `10000.0f` threshold literals gave the prior exact result. The fade multiplier is the shared `DAT_00570c20` global.
