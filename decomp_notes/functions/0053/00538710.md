## Pass 2 wrap-alpha-092

The fresh current-TU baseline was 1111 effective bytes versus 1135 original,
1051 strict differences, 81.07% shape similarity, a 0x28 frame, three returns,
and all 11 direct calls aligned.

The executable reads the packed tile byte at `RGE_Tile+0x6`, masks its low
nibble for the border index, and shifts its high nibble for the border shape.
`orig/ghidra_decomp/map.cpp.decomp` also shows `get_border_type` storing
`(border & 0xf) | (edge << 4)` in that byte. The current `map.h` layout proves
that `border_type` and `border_shape` share this byte. Thus a read through the
four-bit `tile->border_type` field cannot supply the high nibble.

The retained source reads this proven packed byte through a byte pointer and
uses `border_info >> 4` for all four border helper calls. The current PDB type
does not expose the high nibble as a named field. This evidence-backed raw
access repairs the border-shape behavior and keeps the strongest codegen basin.
The final target check is 1108 effective bytes, 1049 strict differences,
69.87% resync similarity, 81.07% shape similarity, a 0x28 frame, three
returns, and 11 aligned direct calls. The size gap is 27 bytes.

1044 differences and 64.27% shape similarity. A cached border-shape local
emitted 1077 bytes with 1048 differences and 71.20% shape similarity. Both
