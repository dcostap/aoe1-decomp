- `004BA18C`: `mov esi,[esp+10h]` discards the final pixel cursor.
- `004BA190`: `mov ecx,[esp+18h]` loads pitch.
- `004BA198`: `add esi,ecx` forms the next row start.
- `004BA19B`: `mov [esp+10h],esi` saves that start.
